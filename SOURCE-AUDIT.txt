same geometry, and the interface explicitly identifies the shared trace.

no unverified emergency-only internal corridor is created. The separate
emergency slide reads triage, examination, staff response and observation.
Site approach paths follow visible roads and the team's entrance positions.
BMT approach paths follow visible passages to the controlled threshold and
support rooms. These are analytical role relationships, not certified protocols.

claiming measured distances, construction details or operating performance.
Bubble areas are schematic. No floor-area values are inferred from circle size.
